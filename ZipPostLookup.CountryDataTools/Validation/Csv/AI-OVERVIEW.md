# Pipeline — AI Overview

_Generated by automated architecture audit. Snapshot of structure & concerns, not authoritative spec._

**Role:** CSV-validation domain support used by the file-based `validate` / `fix` / `extract-issues` commands. Not superseded — distinct from the DB-side `Validation/` rules and the analysis passes.

## Files
| File | Responsibility | Notes / concerns |
|---|---|---|
| `ValidationRules.cs` | Runs all structural/per-row/cross-row checks over a parsed `CsvRow` list → `ValidationError[]` | **Live**: called by ValidateCommand, FixCommand, ExtractIssuesCommand. Has its own `GetRules` switch returning library `ICountryCodeRules` (US/CA/MX) — a third country switch alongside `CountryRulesFactory` and `StateResolver`. Mutates `row.Timezone` as a side effect mid-validation (surprising). |
| `StateResolver.cs` | Resolves canonical (USPS code, name) from ANSI/USPS/full-name, from embedded `us_info.json` | **Live**: used by 5 enrichment API impls. US-only despite living in generic `Pipeline`. |
| `ZipCityComparer.cs` | Case-insensitive `(Code,Name)` equality comparer | **Live**: used by `ValidationRules` and `DSV/Fixer`. XML doc-comment claims it's shared with `Merger` — **`Merger` does not exist** (stale comment / deleted class). |

## Structural & duplication concerns
- **No dead code** here — all three types have callers.
- Naming overlap: `Pipeline/ValidationRules` vs `Reporting/ValidationError` vs `Validation/` namespace — three different "validation" concepts in three namespaces. `ValidationRules` arguably belongs under `Validation/` (or a `Validation/Csv/` subfolder) given the `Validation/Export` precedent.
- `StateResolver` is US-specific but sits in the neutral `Pipeline` namespace; `ZipCityComparer` references a non-existent `Merger` in its doc.
- `ValidationRules.GetRules` is a 3rd hardcoded US/CA/MX country switch in the codebase.

## Recommendations
- Fix the stale `Merger` reference in `ZipCityComparer`'s comment.
- Consider relocating `ValidationRules` to `Validation/` to consolidate the "validation" concept; relocate `StateResolver` to `Validation/Us/` or rename to signal US scope.
- Remove the in-validation `row.Timezone` mutation; do field rewriting in `Fixer`, not the read-only validator.
